# Attributes
Attributes represent your characters most basic strengths and weaknesses. They encompass Intelligence, fitness and natural social adeptness. 

> [!tip] Attribute descriptions
> | Attribute | Full name    | Usage                                  |
> | --------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
> | WIT       | Wit          | Intelligence, intuition and perception |
> | CHA       | Charisma     | Social interaction, empathy            |
> | WIL       | Will         | Mental fortitude, concentration        |
> | FIN       | Finesse      | Art, crafts, instruments               |
> | CON       | Constitution | Strength, endurance                    |
> | MOT       | Motor skills | Reflexes, agility, coordination        |
> | AUT | Authority | Casting magic |

# Additional stats
## Speed
Speed is used during combat to define how far a character can move. It also represents the maximum running speed in meters per second.
**Leap, Jump**
From a sprinting start, you can **jump** your 3 x SPEED in meters. From standing you can **leap** your SPEED in meters.

## Size
Size ranges from -6 to +6, where humans have a size of 0. It has some influence on CON and stealth during combat, as described [[Combat/index#Size and Constitution/Stealth|here]].

# Resources

## Endurance
Endurance can optionally (meaning that the DM can decide to use or ignore the whole mechanic) be used to pay for combat actions. This creates some balance between stronger and weaker actions. 

## Blood points and Consciousness
Blood points introduce a mechanism that can lead to death by bleeding out. Similarly loosing consciousness can be lethal. For details check out the [[Conditions and Character state]] section.

## Physical and mental resistance
The physical resistance describes how many light wounds need to be accumulated to deal a severe wound. The mental resistance is the equivalent for mental conditions (how many points have to accumulate to gain one level).

# Influence
Influence represents your social status, connections and access to money. This doesn't only include high society but also underground dealers and all other connections. This stat is only important if you use the system of influence instead of counting money. More about this can be found in the [[Items and Influence/index|Items and Influence]] section.

## Source Points
Source points represent the resource for casting [[Magic/index|Magic]]. 

## Channel capacity
This limits how much source points can be spent per round

# Skills
Skills represent specifically trained processes. 
## Basic Skills
Basic skills are skills that basically everybody encounters during a normal life. It is a recommendation that every character has these basic skills written down (even if value is 0). 
- Perception
- General knowledge
- Insight
- Self-control
- Animal handling
- Investigation
- Rhetoric
- Athletics
- Stealth
- Nature
- Medicine
- Performance
## Professional Skills
These can be basically everything. A good rule is to avoid highly specific or to broad skills but in the end it's up to the DM if they allow the skill.

# Traits and scars
These characteristics can be vastly different from setting to setting. Or even between characters. 
## Traits
Traits describe special abilities of the character. Traits are a way for the DM to create their own races with distinct features. 
**Postive trait options**
- Advantage on a specialization of a basic skill (eg. Good looking | Advantage on rhethoric:charm)
- Resistance to a specific type of [[Combat/index#Damage Types|Damage]]
- +1 to one attribute
- +1 to physical Resistance or mental Resistance
- +2 to Bloodpoints or Consciousness
- +5 Endurance or Source Points
**Negative trait options**
- Disadvantage on a specialization of a basic skill
-  -1 to one attribute
- Vulnerability to a specific type of [[Combat/index#Damage Types|Damage]]
- -1 to physical Resistance or mental Resistance
- -2 to Bloodpoints or Consciousness
- -5 Endurance or Source Points

## Scars
Scars come from severe wounds. Even when healed they continue impeding the character, even in high fantasy settings where people are magically healed there could be visible scars left on their skin. In more serious settings Scars will give the character a permanent penalty. 
Physical scars have a permanent malus on the character while mental scars only apply when their trigger is met. Then the character has to succeed a roll on self-control against the Level of the scar or be taken over by their illness.
**Physical scar options**
- -1 on one Attribute or Speed per Level
- -3 on Endurance or Source Points per Level
**Mental scar options**
- Mental illness (eg. halluzinations(3), trigger: long isolation)

# Rank and IP
