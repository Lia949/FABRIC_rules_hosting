# Prosthesis
Prosthesis are directly attached to your characters body. They can consist of replacement limbs, subdermal armor, neurolinks or whatever fits your world. Depending on the technology of your world they can give extra boost to skills or attributes or even be necessary for having certain skills (eg. neural access to the net).  

# Equipment 
OP stands for obstruction penalty. Take the sum of your equipment OP values and round
it. If it is bigger than your CON value take the difference as level of obstruction. More about conditions [[Conditions and Character state|here]].
