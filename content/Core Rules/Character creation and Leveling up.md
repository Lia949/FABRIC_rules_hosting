>[!note] Just some rambling
>At first I wanted to make character creation a simple table with some creation points, that could be spent on increasing attributes or skills. Easy, understandable and totally boring.
>
>Every character has their story. Nothing feels worse (to me) during roleplay, than having players not even try to come up with a backstory. DnD for example doesn't really give you a huge impulse to define your past beyond taking some background that maybe gives you some features and starting equipment. I didn't want to do even worse.
>
>I once started to play a campaign in the Witcher TTRPG and I really fell in love with the character creation. After picking a race you had the option to create your unique lifepath by rolling on tables about culture, family fate, social connections, life events and so on. If the result didn't fit, you could of course pick your path through. While some players reached this level of detail in their backstory anyway, I was pleasently surprised that even players that normally didn't bother having any NPC in their backstory suddenly had siblings, former romances and heated rivalries. 
>Of course this style of play is not for everyone, so work out what's best for you. 
>
>Anyway I got inspired to do more than a mere table. Still creation should work similar for any genre or setting, so the options are not as specific as in the Witcher TTRPG.

This chapter is a step-by-step guide on how to create your character. Some good advice is to note down your choices and only stat filling out the charactersheet once you're done and happy with the result. 
# Step 0: Pick your Rank of Power
Thie power rank is provided by the DM as it goes hand in hand with the [[World creation#Difficulty|Difficulty]] of the setting. While the difficulty describes how deadly the setting is, the [[World creation#Power Rank|Power Rank]] describes how it feels to play the character. Do they have to struggle with every step of increasing their strength or are new abilities coming easily.
Power changes both character creation and leveling up. So in two different power ranks can feel quite different at the same level.

# Step 1: Race and Culture

## Starting Stats

### Fixed values
- **Attributes**
  All attributes (WIT, WIL, CHA, CON, FIN, MOT, AUT) start at value 0
- **Bloodpoints**: 10
- **Consciousness**: 10
- **Speed**: 2
- **Endurance**: 10
- **Size**: -2, -1, 0 or 1 (players choice, check [[Combat/index#Size and Constitution/Stealth|size table]] for reference)
- **Influence**: 0
- **Mental and physical Resistance**: 3 each
- **Channel Capacity**: 0
- **Source Points**: 0

### Upgrading Stats
- **Attribute points**
  Used to increase any attribute, Speed or Channel Capacity. Values from 0-3 cost one point per increase, from 3-6 cost two points
- **Skill points**
  Used to increase skill values. Values from 0-6 cost one point, from 6-9 two points and from 9-12 three points.
- **Resistance points**
  Used to increase Mental Resistance, Physical Resistance by one per point or Bloodpoints, Consciousness by two per point
- **Resource points**
  Used to increase Endurance or Source Points by one per point

This table indicated the inital upgrades that can be made, depending on the Power Rank:

|                   | Limited | Mundane | Supernatural | Potent | Mythic |
| ----------------- | ------- | ------- | ------------ | ------ | ------ |
| Attribute points  | 10      | 12      | 14           | 16     | 18     |
| Resistance points | 2       | 3       | 4            | 5      | 6      |
| Resource points   | 2       | 4       | 6            | 8      | 10     |
| Skill points      | 6       | 8       | 10           | 12     | 14     |

## Race
Each race has either two positive and one negative [[Characterstats#Traits|Traits]] or one positive Trait. The DM can either have fixed races or the player finds plausible traits for their chosen race.
## Personal trait
Each character gets one [[Characterstats#Traits|Traits]] of the type that gives advantage on the specialization of a basic skill. Also they get two professional skills with a level of 1.

# Step 4: Childhood and Family Fate
This can be either rolled or the player creates a character backstory freely. Together with the DM a level of Influence is decided upon.
## Social status of family
| Roll | Status             | Effect       |
| ---- | ------------------ | ------------ |
| 1    | extremely poor     | -            |
| 2    | poor               | +1 Influence |
| 3    | managing           | +2 Influence |
| 4    | middle-class       | +3 Influence |
| 5    | upper middle-class | +4 Influence |
| 6    | rich               | +5 Influence |

## Family Relations
Roll on the table 

| Roll | Family relationship                     | Childhood                     | Effect                         |
| ---- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1    | extremely bad relation or none left     | Extremely hard                | +1 Scar, +1 self-control       |
| 2    | bad relation, missed people or few left | Hard                          | +1 self-control                |
| 3    | Some good, some bad                     | Sometimes good, sometimes bad | +1 performance                 |
| 4    | Mostly good                             | Mostly good                   | +1 Rhethoric                   |
| 5    | Good relations                          | Carefree                      | +1 Insight                     |
| 6    | very close and loving relations         | Extremely good                | +1 Attribute point, +1 Insight |

## Siblings
Roll 1d6. On a 1-2 you're an only child. On a 3-6 you have siblings, roll 1d6 to see how many. For each sibling roll on the table to see how close you are/were.

| Roll | Sibling relationship           |
| ---- | ------------------------------ |
| 1    | extremely bad relation         |
| 2    | bad relation or no contact     |
| 3    | Some good, some bad            |
| 4    | Mostly good                    |
| 5    | Good relations                 |
| 6    | very close and loving relation |

# Step 6: Life Events
Roll 1d6 for every full decade of age. For an even result roll with 2d6 on the Fortune table, for uneven on the Misfortune. 

| Roll | Fortune (even)                                                                                  | Misfortune (uneven)                                   |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 2    | A Noble of Influence 8 owes you                                                                 | Near-death event, +1 Scar                             |
| 3    | Jackpot: money in the value of a level 6 item                                                   | Got indepted with a Level of 7                        |
| 4    | You found a manual with some useful insight for a skill. Gain 2 skill points.                   | You got addicted to something                         |
| 5    | You made a strong positive impression on some small community. They're always happy to see you. | You fell ill for a longer time. Loose 2 Skill points. |
| 6    | A old person got really fond of you and treats you like a grandchild                            | You lost your job                                     |
| 7    | You found something useful. Get a Level 3 item.                                                 | Lost a treasured item                                 |
| 8    | Got a loyal pet animal of Level 3 (see [[Creatures#Simple Creatures\|Simple Creatures]])        | A friend died                                         |
| 9    | You got the chance to travel around a lot. Get +2 in General knowledge                          | You were betrayed by a friend                         |
| 10   | Found a loyal and powerful friend. + 1 Influence                                                | Your house burned down, -1 Influence                  |
| 11   | You got a stipend to study/ be trained at an organization. Get 5 skill points.                  | Made an enemy of someone powerful with Influence 8    |
| 12   | Found a Mentor that trained you. Get 7 skill points                                             | Accused for a crime you didn't do                     |

# Step 7: Friendships and relationships

You can take a dept of Level 7 and go to Influence Level of 3 by joining a criminal organization.

Note at least three most important people in your life. Even if it's the baker next door where you get your morning coffee.

# Step 8: Profession and skills
Decide on what abilities the character spent their life on pursuing. This can include schooling or accelerated teaching as a child as well as studies, learned professions or hobbies. They get Improvement points that can be used like when the character [[#Leveling up|levels up]].

|       | Improvement points per year |
| ----- | --------------------------- |
| Child | 1                           |
| Adult | 2                           |
| Old   | -0.5                        |

**Accelerated learning** means having a dedicated teacher that actively supports the character. This is only possible with enough influence level or taking a dept. For the period of accelerated learning the character gets **double** the Improvement Points than normal.

| Influence Level | Dept Level | Years of accelerated learning possible |     |
| --------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------- | --- |
| 1-3             | 1-5        | 0                                      |     |
| 4               | 6          | 4                                      |     |
| 5               | 7          | 6                                      |     |
| 6               | 8          | 10                                     |     |

# Step 9: Equipment
Additionally to items that you may have gotten during the previous steps, you can have the following Equipment: 
- 2 items of the same level as your influence
- 4 items of one level below your influence
- 6 items of any level, with a max of two below your influence

# Leveling up
For each Level the character gets Improvement Points (IP). The number of IPs based on the Power Rank is given in the following table. 

|                         | Limited | Mundane | Supernatural | Potent | Mythic |
| ----------------------- | ------- | ------- | ------------ | ------ | ------ |
| Improvement Points (IP) | 8       | 10      | 13           | 16     | 20     |
The Improvement Points can be used to buy [[#Upgrading Stats|Upgrade]] points as indicated here:

| Upgrade points     | IP cost                                          |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1 Skill Point      | 1                                                |
| 1 Resource Point   | 1                                                |
| 1 Attribute Point  | 2                                                |
| 1 Resistance Point | 5 (only on special occasions, decided by the DM) |
An alternative way is to not use Levels at all and instead have the DM dealing out IPs as they wish.
