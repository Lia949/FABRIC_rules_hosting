# Discussion/ Haggling

| Level | Quality / Example |
|-------|------------------|
| 1 | Insult, bribery, threat, repetition of previous argument, joke, diversion |
| 2 | General statement, analogy, fallacious argument (argument does not actually fit the topic), accusation, straw man argument (exaggeration of the opposing argument) |
| 3 | Reason, anecdote, emotional influence, complex question |
| 4 | Indictment, sophisticated deception/lie, scandal, revelation |

# Flirt

| Level | Quality / Example |
|-------|------------------|
| 1 | Showing off, allusion, pick-up line, polite small talk |
| 2 | Superficial compliments, simple jokes, relaxed body language, expressing genuine but respectful interest in superficial topics |
| 3 | Genuine compliments, appropriate physical touch, playful banter, asking about personal topics |
| 4 | In-depth conversation, shared humor, genuine emotional connection, high-quality listening |

# Reject

| Level | Quality / Example |
|-------|------------------|
| 1 | Flustered stuttering, awkward silence, laugh |
| 2 | Subtle signals, changing the topic, making a joke |
| 3 | Telling them you're not interested, trying to ignore them, presenting yourself as less desirable by doing something embarrassing |
| 4 | Politely and respectfully explaining your feelings, completely destroying their self-confidence |

# Ridicule

| Level | Quality / Example                                                                                                     |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Light teasing, playful jab, harmless joke, mild sarcasm                                                               |
| 2     | Pointing out obvious flaws, mocking minor mistakes, ironic imitation, dismissive humor                                |
| 3     | Sharp mockery, exposing contradictions, humiliating comparisons                                                       |
| 4     | Brutal verbal takedown, merciless exposure of weaknesses, devastating satire, completely shattering their credibility |
