A round represents around 3 seconds of combat. Each character has one [[Main action]] and
up to two [[Side action]]s.

# Phases in a round
## 1. Player phase
Each Player announces their [[Main action]]. The order of that can be chosen by the players each round. They can also choose to wait after the Opponent phase with choosing their actions.
## 2. Opponent phase
Opponent announces their [[Main action]].
## 3. Reaction phase
Players that didn’t choose an action before can do so now.
## 4. Movement and rolling the checks
Now the round gets resolved and all players roll their dice.
