**Take/drop an item**
- Take an item from the floor (1 Action)
- Take an item from the backpack (2 Actions)

**Taking care of wounds**
- Apply pressure (1 Action)
	No [[Bloodpoints]] are lost this round caused by this wound
- Bandage bleeding wound (1 Action)
	**Requirement:** Have something in hand to bandage with
	**Check:** FIN + medicine 
	**DC:** 1
	**Effect:** On success the bleeding is stopped (but it is still a serious wound)
- Stabilize serious wound (2 Actions)
	**Requirement:** Have something in hand to bandage with
	**Check:** FIN + medicine
	**DC:** 2 x Wound severity
	**Effect:** On success the wound is [[Stabilizing|stabilized]] and bleeding is stopped

**Move**
- Move carefully (1 Action)
	Move 1 m, +2 bonus to sneaking checks
- Sprint (1 Action) 
	Move speed x 3 m
- Tackle (1 Action)
	Move up to speed x 3 m and end the round next to your target.
	**Check:** Compared MOT checks. The attacker can take a +2 bonus on the roll with the risk of falling prone when failing the tackle.
	**Effect:** If successful both fall prone with the attacker on top. 

**Prepare next action/Help**
Can be used to trick, trap or incite someone. Roll an appropriate skill check, the number of successes divided by two can be used as a bonus for the next action against this target.

**Skill check**
The duration depends on the task. If a certain amount of Points has to be reached sum the check
Quality until the threshold is met.
