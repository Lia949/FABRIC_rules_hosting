**Check:** MOT + Ranged combat skill
**DC:** Distance - Target [[Size table|Size]] + Movement + Sight (The target can also [[Side action|Dodge]])
**Quality:** Equal to the close combat the Quality counts as extra damage points. 
**Failure**: If you miss while shooting into a melee it is possible that you hit another person instead. The DM will declare a roll for the probability of that.
**Hit location:** To determine the hit body part roll on the [[Hit location table]] (if you didn't pick a specific limb).

# Actions
- Standard ranged attack (1 Action):
	Roll a ranged combat skill against the target difficulty. 
- Aim (1 Action):
	Difficulty is lowered by 2. Can be only taken two times in a row.
- Fast fire (1 Action): 
	Only for weapons with fast fire possibility. Roll ranged attacks against X targets. On top of normal difficulty of each target add X for each shot.
- Reload (X Actions):
	X depends on the weapon. Make a check on <FIN> weapon skill to shorten the reload action by Quality/2, with a minimum duration of 1. Some weapons can be reloaded during a side action
