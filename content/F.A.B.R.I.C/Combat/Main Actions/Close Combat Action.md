Close combat checks use Motor skills as base attribute together with the chosen combat skill, which is compared to the enemies check (or level). On a successful check the player deals his weapon damage as well as the Quality of the check as additional damage. If they fail they take the weapon damage of their opponent as well as the (negative) Quality. If the difficulty is exactly met both parties deal their weapon damage.
To determine the hit body part roll on the [[Hit location table]] (if you didn't pick aimed attack).
# Armed Actions
- Standard combat action
	**Check:** MOT + marital skill. 
	**DC:** The difficulty for the check is the level of the opponent. 
	**Effect:** On a successful check the player deals his weapon damage plus the Quality of the check as additional damage. If they fail they take the weapon damage of their opponent as well as the (negative) Quality. If the difficulty is exactly met both parties deal their weapon damage.
- Defensive/Offensive combat action:
	Splits the Quality of your combat check into Defense and Attack. The Attack has -X and the Defense +X (or the other way round). X can be chosen from 1 - CON and has to be declared before the check.
- Push back/Knock over:
	If your combat check is successful the Quality doesn’t add extra damage but acts as bonus for a CON check against your opponent. If successful they are pushed back the Quality in meters/knocked down.
- Steal item/Disarm:
	If your combat check is successful the Quality doesn’t add extra damage but acts as bonus for a MOT check against your opponent. If successful you can take the item/the opponent is disarmed.
- Aimed attack:
	Penalty of -2 on the check for hitting head or torso. Penalty of -1 to hit a specific limb.
- Swipe attack: 
	Attack all enemies surrounding you at once. Take the normal penalty for fighting multiple enemies. Every enemy individually dodges/counterattacks. Your defense is lowered by one for each targeted enemy.
- Charge attack: 
	**Requirement:** Sprint in previous [[Main action]]
	Do a standard attack, if you're successful deal your speed as additional damage. On a failure you take half of your speed as additional damage

# Unarmed Actions
Unarmed attacks use MOT or CON as base attribute. It is allowed to hold a weapon in one hand while doing an unarmed attack (for kicks two-handed weapons are ok). Fighting unarmed against an armed opponent gives a penalty of -2 (on top of the distance penalty). Hitting/kicking armor without having armor gives you half the damage you delt. Grappling actions give their Quality as bonus to the next grappling action. The opponent can break free if they win the compared grappling check.

- Kick/Punch:
	check on MOT/CON + unarmed martial skill. The dealt damage is equal to the Quality of the check.
- Grab (grappling):
	If successful the opponent has the restrained conditions (see below for details). The opponent can break free if they win the compared grappling check.
- Strangle (grappling): 
	Only from grappling or from a sneak attack. If successful the opponent gets the restrained condition. After 3 rounds of uninterrupted strangling the opponent gets the unconscious condition for the next X rounds. X is the number of successes after rolling 6 D6.
- Throw (grappling): 
	Only from grappling. If succesful the opponent is prone and takes damage equal to the Quality.
- Pin (grappling): 
	Only against prone opponent. If successful the target gains the immobilized condition.
