Hitting a Critical Location doubles the damage. Close combat attacks suffer a -4 penalty for attacking a critical point (using the aimed attack action). For ranged actions it depends on the size of the chosen location.
~~Before deciding to hit there roll a check against the indicated DC to see if your character knows the location.~~

**Critical Locations:**
- Heart (DC 2)
- Eye (DC 1)
- Spleen (DC 3)
- Spinal cord (DC 3)
