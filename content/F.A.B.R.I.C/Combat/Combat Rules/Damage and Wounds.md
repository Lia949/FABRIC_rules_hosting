Each point of damage causes a [[Light Wound]].  If the number of light wound on a limb reach the [[Wound Threshold]] these light wounds are replaced with one [[Severe Wound]].
Having [[Severe Wound]]s can lead to [[Severe Wound#Wound Obstruction Penalty|Wound Obstruction Penalty]] and [[Severe Wound#Unconsciousness|Unconsciousness]].

**Bleeding wounds**
For each severe wound that was fully caused by one cutting or piercing attack roll one D6, on a success it causes a [[Bleeding Wound]]. 

**Knock out**
For each severe wound that was fully caused by one blunt attack on the head roll one D6, on a success the attack knocks your opponent out for 1D6 combat rounds.

# Resistance
A resistance halves the damage of a certain kind.

# Immunity
A immunity prevents damage of a certain kind.

# Vulnerability 
A vulnerability to a damage type doubles the damage from that source.
