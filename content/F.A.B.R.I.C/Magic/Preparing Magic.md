To prepare magic it is possible to use materials, magic runes or circles and extended casting times. Also more than one caster can be used to increase the chance of succeeding. ^b3fd92

## Materials
Materials are usually used/destroyed by the ritual and lower the [[Basic Attributes#Source Points|Source Point]] cost. The DM has to define which materials or items can be used for the ritual. Generally it should be items/materials that are somewhat hard to find.
> [!tip] Source Points = Item Level

## Preparations
Preparation in terms of everything from a quick rune to a meticulously drawn summoning circle. Roll a **FIN + "rune drawing skill"** check against the Preparation check DC. If it is successful the Spell DC is reduced by the value given in the table. 
For the Preparations with a DC of 10 or higher written instructions are necessary, otherwise a **WIT + "rune drawing skill"** check against **quarter of the Effect Level** of the spell has to be rolled.
If the preparation check fails, the spell DC is not reduced. 

| Preparation check DC | Preparation time        | Reduction of Spell DC |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------- |
| 2                    | 1 Action                | 3                     |
| 2                    | 3 Actions               | 5                     |
| 4                    | 5 Actions               | 8                     |
| 6                    | 10 Actions (30 seconds) | 12                    |
| 8                    | 20 Actions (1 minute)   | 16                    |
| 10                   | 5 minutes               | 20                    |
| 12                   | 15 minutes              | 24                    |
| 15                   | 30 minutes              | 30                    |
| 15                   | 1 hour                  | 35                    |
> [!note] Maybe add difficulty of Effect Level in some way in check? Quarter of it? Table?


# Increasing Casting time
Increasing casting time gives a bonus to the [[Casting Magic#Power check|Power check]]. Furthermore it is useful to prevent [[Casting Magic#Magic Recoil|Magic Recoil]], as the Source Points spent per round equals the total amount divided by the number of Actions used to cast.

| Additional Casting Time | Bonus |
| ----------------------- | ----- |
| 1 Action                | +2    |
| 2 Actions               | +4    |
| 3 Actions               | +6    |
| 5 Actions               | +8    |
| 8 Actions               | +10   |

# Multiple Casters
Having multiple casters lowers the spell DC and allows for [[Basic Attributes#Source Points|Source Point]] costs to be split (doesn't have to be evenly). However if one caster fails the whole spell [[Casting Magic#Failure|fails]]. 
It is possible to have passive participants that only contribute Source Points without casting. 

| Number of Casters | Reduction of Spell DC |
| ----------------- | --------------------- |
| 2                 | 3                     |
| 3                 | 5                     |
| 5                 | 8                     |
| 10                | 12                    |
| 20                | 16                    |
| 40                | 20                    |
| 70                | 25                    |
| 100               | 30                    |
