Information
- Message: Effect Level rises by 1 for every five words. (Duration: Instant)
- Communicate (eg. animals, other languages): Effect Level is 1 for conveying few simple
emotions and 6 for perfect understanding (Duration: 1 round)
- Scry: Effect Level is 2 for vague glimpse and 12 for perfect image, Level increases in
steps of two (Duration: 1 round)
- Distance call: Effect Level is 3 for vague emotions and 18 for perfect image and sound,
Level increases in steps of three (Duration: 1 round)
- See Future/Past: Effect Level is 6*Information Levle for seeing the future and
3*Information Level for seeing the past, where Information Level goes from 1: vague
glimpse to 6: clear image (Duration: Instant)

# Message
See [[#Communicate|Communication Level]] and [[Casting Magic#2. Ranged Magic (Side action)|Distance Level]].
> [!danger] **Effect Level   =   Communication Level   x   Distance Level**

# Communicate
Use this to talk to animals or communicate in other languages. 
> [!danger] **Effect Level**
> | Level | Description |
> | --- | ---|
> | 1 | Simple emotions |
> | 2 | Complex emotions |
> | 3 | impressions of things |
> | 4 | monosyllabic words |
> | 5 | Simple sentences |
> | 6 | Perfect Communication |

# Distance Call
See [[#Communicate|Communication Level]] and [[Casting Magic#2. Ranged Magic (Side action)|Distance Level]]
> [!danger] **Effect Level   =   (Communication Level  + Sound Level  +  Image Level)  x   Distance Level**

> [!danger] **Sound Level**
> | Level | Description |
> | --- | ---|
> | 1 | Only really loud sounds get through |
> | 2 | Only few words can be yelled through a lot of noise |
> | 3 | Communication possible, but large noise overlaying it |
> | 4 | Communication good, background noise sometimes interferes |
> | 5 | Communication perfect, background sounds suppressed  |
> | 6 | Perfect Sound, as if there |

> [!danger] **Image Level**
> | Level | Description |
> | --- | ---|
> | 1 | Blurry patches of light and dark |
> | 2 | One blurry image every few seconds  |
> | 3 | One image every second |
> | 4 | Blurry video |
> | 5 | Good video, slightly pixly  |
> | 6 | Perfect Video, as if there |

# Observation
In principle the same as [[#Distance Call]] but the information is only transferred one way. The Quality of the check has to be above the targets protection to be spied upon to work. 
> [!danger] **Effect Level   =   ${\Huge \frac{\text{Communication Level}  + \text{Sound Level}  +  \text{Image Level}}{2}}$   x   Distance Level**
