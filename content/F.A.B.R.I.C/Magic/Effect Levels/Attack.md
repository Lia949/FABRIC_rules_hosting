# Standard Attack
This attack can be used as a ranged spell or as a weapon that is used for a melee attack. The Area is rounded up to full square meters, thus the minimum Area is 1, even if the attack is much smaller. 
> [!danger] **Effect Level   =   Damage   x   Area**

# Push/Pull
When hit the target has to make a check against the Effect Level. If they fail they can be pushed/pulled by 5m for each Level of Quality.
> [!danger] ** CON check against Effect Level**

# Trip
When hit the target has to make a check against the Effect Level. If they fail they are knocked prone.
> [!danger] ** CON or MOT check against Effect Level**

# Disarm
When hit the target has to make a check against the Effect Level. If they fail they loose their weapon.
> [!danger] ** CON or MOT check against Effect Level**

# Steal Item
When hit the target has to make a check against the Effect Level. If they fail they lose the item.
> [!danger] **MOT check against Effect Level**
