# Effect Level
Each of Magic Effect states so called Effect Levels, that describe the overall power and difficulty of the cast magic. Its important to note that categories are not bound to schools of magic. It doesn’t matter if an attack is made with ice magic, necrotic touch or something entirely else. Defining which schools of magic exist and what categories are possible to use with them is entirely up to the creator of the world.
