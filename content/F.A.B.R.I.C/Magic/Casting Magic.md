> [!tip] Magic skills
> To cast magic you need some kind of magic skill. This skill /skills will vary between different genres or worlds (see [[Magic System]]), thus it will simply be called "magic skill" in the following.

# Failure
If any magic check fails you pay only half of the [[Basic Attributes#Source Points|Source Points]] (but Recoil damage is still taken).

# 1a. Power check (Main action/Side action)
Roll a check on [[Basic Attributes#Power|Power]] + "magic skill" against the desired [[Magic Effect#Effect Level|Effect Level]]. Normally casting takes one [[Main action]] but you can decide how long you take to cast it (see [[Preparing Magic]]). [[Spell Proficiency]] can give you a bonus on the casting or even allow you to cast as a [[Side action]]. Pay the amount of [[Basic Attributes#Source Points|Source Points]] defined by the Effect. 

# 1b. Magic Recoil
If the total number of Source Points spent in one round is above your [[Basic Attributes#Channel Capacity|Channel Capacity]] roll the difference amount of D6s. All failures cause one minor wound, starting (evenly split) with the body parts(s) that channels the most magic. When the damage reaches beyond the Wound Threshold, the remaining damage spreads to the adjacent parts. 

# 2. Ranged Magic (Side action)
Whenever you're casting from a distance and cannot touch the target roll a check on [[Basic Attributes#Control|Control]] + "magic skill" against the Target Difficulty, which is calculated like this:
> [!tip] Target DC  =  Distance Level  +  Movement  +  Sight  +  Size  +  Dodge/WIL

Dodge is used for physical Attacks, WIL for mental ones. If the Effect is an Attack, the [[Basic Rules#Quality|Quality]] deals extra damage.
[[Spell Proficiency]] can give a bonus on this check.
> [!tip] **Distance**
> | Level | Description |  | Level | Description |
> | --- | ---| --- | --- | ---|
> | 1 | 2 - 5 m | | 7 | 1 - 5 km |
> | 2 | 5 - 15 m | | 8 | 5 - 15 km |
> | 3 | 15 - 50 m | | 9 | 15 - 50 km |
> | 4 | 50 - 100 m |  | 10 | 50 - 100 km |
> | 5 | 100 - 500 m | | 11 | 100 - 500 km |
> | 6 | 500 - 1000 m  |  | 12 | anywhere |

# 3. Concentration (Side action)
If the [[Magic Effect]]s duration is longer than one action, Concentration has to  be upheld in the following rounds. This counts as one [[Side action]]. Roll a check on [[Basic Attributes#Control|Control]] + "magic skill" against half of the [[Magic Effect#Effect Level|Effect Level]]. If it succeeds the Effect continues and you pay the Effect Level in Source Points. 
Note that the Target can also break free of the [[Magic Effect]] during a [[Main action]] as stated in its description.
