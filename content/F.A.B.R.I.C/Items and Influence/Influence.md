Instead of money each character has influence, which represents social status, money and connections. 
To be able to buy/borrow/get an item the character has to have an influence of at least the [[Item Level]]. Purchasing an item of the same level as the influence should happen rarely or else the level of influence will decrease. 
Finding treasures or doing favors increases Influence. This concept is very vague so its important that GM and players cooperate. Of course it is always possible to stick to the usual money-bookkeeping system or using the column that says how much money per week can be spent.
