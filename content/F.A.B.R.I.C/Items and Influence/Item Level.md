The Item Level tells how strong, valuable or useful an item is. For each item the Level is equal to the sum of [[Artistry Value]], [[Enchantment Value]] and [[Physical Value]]. Some items are heavy or impractical to handle, these impose an [[Obstruction Penalty]].

The following table gives some examples for Item Levels to get a feeling for wealth. Depending on your [[Influence]], Items can be acquired. This system is an alternative to commonly used money-based systems.

| 1          | Food, simple clothes, simple tools    |
| 2          | Simple Weapons                        |
| 3          | Good clothes                          |
| 4          | Good weapons                          |
| 5          | Jewelry, very good weapons            |
| 6          | tailor-made clothes                   |
| 7          | Art, simple vehicle                   |
| 8          | Special equipment                     |
| 9          | Valuable jewelry, Good vehicle        |
| 10         | Fancy vehicle, Simple House, Servant  |

| 12         | Fancy house, collection of arts       |
| 13         | A ship, Valuable artifacts,           |
| 14         | Legendary artefact, Villa             |
| 15         | Palace, magnificent three-masted ship |
| 16         | Personal army                         |
| 17         | A City                                |
