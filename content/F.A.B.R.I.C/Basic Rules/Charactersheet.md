# Wounds, Bloodpoints and Insanity
Each body part can be wounded and if it happens the character gets a penalty for dice
rolls that use that body part. Bloodpoints are important for bleeding wounds. Yes, you
can die from that. In certain settings it is possible to get insane. Each level in madness
adds a mental disorder to your character.

# Speed, Wound threshold and Influence
Speed actually describes your top sprinting speed in m/s. It is also the value in m that
you can go in 1 combat round with basic movement. The wound threshold describes, how
many small wound have to accumulate on a body part to suffer a severe wound. Influence
represents your social status, connections and access to money.

# Equipment OP and wound OP
OP stands for obstruction penalty. Take the sum of your equipment OP values and round
it. If it is bigger than your CON value take the difference as penalty. The wound OP is the
amount of severe wounds divided by 2.
