Attributes represent your characters most basic strengths and weaknesses. They encompass Intelligence, fitness and natural social adeptness. 
# Wit 
**Short form**: WIT
**Used for**:  intelligence and perception.
# Charisma 
**Short form:** CHA
**Used for:** Social interaction, empathy.
# Will 
**Short form:** WIL
**Used for:** Mental fortitude, concentration.
# Finesse 
**Short form:** FIN
**Used for:** Crafts, instruments.
# Constitution 
**Short form:** CON
**Used for:** Strength, endurance.
# Motor skills 
**Short form:** MOT
**Used for:** Reflexes, agility, coordination, combat
