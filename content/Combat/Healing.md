# Stabilizing wounds
Stabilizing serious wounds removes the pain condition of that wound. But stabilized wounds still count towards the maximum per body part before it is lost. Stabilizing wounds during combat (also works for bleeding wounds) is described in [[Main Actions]].  
Out of combat it is expected that you have enough time to properly stabilize all wounds (still needing some material for bandaging, but that is left to role play). Think of it as what first aid is in real life, everybody should be able to do it. 

# Healing
Healing is a longer process and happens during role play (as it is heavily dependent on the setting), even if something is healed with magic the effect should not be instant. Some settings might have superior items that enhance healing and the recovery of resources however.

Even if a severe wound is healed there can be permanent damage. As a rule of thumb each severe wound creates a visible scar with no further effect. Two severe wounds at one body part can lead to a permanent penalty, while three severe wounds lead to a lost limb. The longlasting effects are noted down under [[Characterstats#Traits, scars and prothesis|Traits, scars and prothesis]]. The DM should decide on the penalty of the scar.

Magic or futuristic medicine can be used to avoid this permanent damage.

## Recovering Resources
Bloodpoints, Consciousness, Source points and Endurance recover automatically over time, as described in [[Conditions and Character state#Resource recovery|Resource recovery]]. However medical treatment or potions can quicken this process. The specifics are left to roleplay.

## Therapy
Insanity points can be recovered with an successful check in psychology (made by the therapist) during a 10 minute therapy in safe environment. The DC is the current level of insanity + 1. The Quality of the check represents the points of insanity that are lowered.   
The level in Insanity can only be lowered by a longer psychological treatment (the corresponding psychological disorder is healed) and is left to role play. 

Once level 6 is reached the character is fully insane and cannot be controlled by the player any more. The DM plays the character from now on.
