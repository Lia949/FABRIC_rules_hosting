This chapter presents an alternate system to tracking money and inventing price lists for fictional items, which in my opinion is really tedious (especially as a DM). Instead of money, each character has influence, which represents social status, money and connections. 
To be able to buy/borrow/get an item the character has to have an influence of at least the [[Items]]. Purchasing an item of the same level as the influence should happen rarely or else the level of influence will decrease. 
Finding treasures or doing favors increases or at least holds Influence. Doing nothing causes the Influence to fall, as there is still a constant income necessary to keep it. Owning a business might circumvent this. 

The concept of influence is very vague so its important that GM and players cooperate. Of course it is always possible to stick to the usual money-bookkeeping system or using the column that says how much money per week can be spent. GP stands for gold piece but can of course be translated to any currency that fits your setting.

# Depts
A dept of a certain level means that the character has to pay back value equal to that item Level. Again it is more of a vague system and depts can be reduced partially or fully during roleplay, when the DM agrees that the character gave back enough for that.
